Common questions

Questions families ask

Straight answers for this demo. A real clinic can keep the questions and replace the sample details.

Is Demo Clinic, Baner a real clinic?

No. Demo Clinic, Baner is a fictional clinic created for this sample website. Zenetic (zenetic.in), a digital agency in Pune, uses the page to show real clinics how their own site could look. The address, phone number, doctors, timings and fees are placeholders.

Where is the clinic shown on this page?

The sample address is Shop 12, Sample Plaza, Baner Road, Baner, Pune, Maharashtra 411045. The map is centred on the Baner area of Pune in general. It is not a pin for a real clinic.

What are the clinic timings?

Sample hours are Monday to Friday, 9:00 am to 1:00 pm and 5:00 pm to 8:30 pm; Saturday, 9:00 am to 1:00 pm and 5:00 pm to 8:00 pm; and Sunday, 10:00 am to 1:00 pm. These times are for the demo layout only.

Do you accept walk-in patients?

On this sample timetable, morning hours for the general physician are described as open to walk-ins. Paediatrics, women's health and physiotherapy are shown as appointment-first. A real clinic would confirm availability on the day. This demo cannot take a walk-in.

How do I book an appointment?

Use the appointment request form on this page, or the Call and WhatsApp links. The form only checks that the fields look complete. Submitting it does not send or store your details. You can then choose to open WhatsApp with the same details, still using the sample number +91 90000 00000.

Do you offer cashless insurance?

This demo does not list any real insurer or cashless tie-up. A clinic website might say that some cashless facilities can be arranged, and that patients should confirm the insurer, the treatment and the approval before the visit. Cash, card and UPI are shown here only as sample payment methods.

Is parking available?

The fictional address mentions visitor parking in the basement of Sample Plaza. That note exists for the layout only. It is not a real car park, and this page cannot reserve a bay.

Do you collect lab samples from home?

Home sample collection is listed as a sample service for Baner and nearby parts of Pune, with a morning window of 7:00 am to 10:00 am. This website does not collect samples, book a phlebotomist or share results.

Which departments are listed?

The sample departments are general physician, paediatrics, women's health, diabetes and thyroid care, physiotherapy, diagnostics and lab, vaccination, and health check-up packages.

What should I bring, and how would I pay?

A real clinic visit usually needs a photo ID, earlier reports and a list of current medicines. You do not need to bring anything to use this demo. Sample payment methods shown are cash, card and UPI, with placeholder fees in rupees that are not real charges.
